# Contributing

IKHMAD is currently developed under a controlled project structure.

Public issues, suggestions and documentation corrections may be accepted in the future. Source-code contributions should not be submitted unless a contribution process is explicitly opened.

Contributors must not submit:
- confidential institutional information;
- personal emergency data;
- proprietary third-party code;
- credentials;
- patent-sensitive or trade-secret material.

Any future external contributor should work under written terms addressing confidentiality and intellectual-property ownership before contributing to proprietary IKHMAD components.
