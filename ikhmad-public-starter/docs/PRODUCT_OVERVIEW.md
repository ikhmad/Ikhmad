# Product Overview

## Problem

Wildfire response involves fragmented information, delayed reporting, rapidly changing hazards, uncertain road safety and difficult coordination between citizens and professional responders.

## IKHMAD approach

IKHMAD is designed as a shared wildfire intelligence and emergency coordination layer that brings together:

1. citizen reporting;
2. professional incident validation;
3. geospatial situational awareness;
4. multi-source incident information;
5. targeted alerts;
6. SOS requests;
7. evacuation decision support;
8. future predictive routing and safe-zone recommendation.

## Primary users

### Citizens
Receive local risk information, report hazards, receive warnings, send SOS requests and follow evacuation guidance.

### Operators / authorities
Review incidents, validate reports, monitor risk, coordinate alerts, assess affected areas and support operational decisions.

## Product principle

IKHMAD should not be understood as only a fire-reporting application. Reporting is one input into a wider intelligence, coordination and evacuation system.
