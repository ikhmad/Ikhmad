# Command-Center Workflow

## Incident intake
1. New incident/report appears in the incoming queue.
2. Operator opens the report.
3. Operator reviews location, evidence and available contextual information.
4. Operator may update validation/state information.
5. Incident becomes visible according to its operational state.

## SOS
1. Incoming SOS appears with location and time.
2. Operator opens the request.
3. Available incident context is displayed.
4. Operator updates response/acknowledgment state.

## Alerts
Operators can prepare or trigger public-facing alert workflows according to authorization and operational policy.

## Decision support
Future versions of IKHMAD will surface outputs from proprietary risk, hazard, routing and safe-zone services. Operators remain responsible for authorized operational decisions.
